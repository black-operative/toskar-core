Ratatoskr

RAH-tah-tosk · "drill-tooth"

In the myth, a squirrel runs up and down the World Tree carrying messages between the eagle at the top and Níðhöggr at the roots. Yggdrasil does the same job: it carries requests between your apps and the models on your computers.

Touring every state. Pick one to stop.

512 px

Every state, live

Each state is a pose the same rig blends into, so switching between them is smooth. They're mapped to things the app already does.

Small sizes

The tufted ears and the curled tail keep the shape readable at icon size, on both your dark and light themes.

ᚨHe lives on the tree

A raven is Odin's bird and could front any Norse-themed product. Ratatoskr lives on Yggdrasil itself, and his whole job is carrying messages. The rune on his forehead is Ansuz, the rune of messages and speech.

ᚱHe stands out on your UI

Your dark theme is near-black, so a black raven fades into it. Copper fur stands out against it and against the light theme. Teal is used only for his eyes and runes, so it still matches your primary color.

ᛒHe's built to animate

The tail is 16 jointed segments, and the arms, ears, eyes and acorn each move on their own. A new animation is a new pose, so nobody has to draw new frames.

ᛗHe carries something

The acorn is the message. It glows while he delivers, he nibbles it while a model thinks, and he drops it when something breaks. That gives each state a prop to act with.

How it's built

He's one inline SVG driven by a small script, with no image files. Each state is a function that returns a pose, and the rig eases toward it every frame. This can be ported to a React component in web/src/components with a state prop. Reduced-motion settings freeze him in a still pose.

  • idle · daemon running, nothing queued
  • greet · first run, onboarding
  • think · chat completion streaming
  • deliver · Bifrost: work placed on a paired computer
  • success · download, install, training deployed
  • error · Heimdall check failing, chat or task error
  • sleep · model unloaded after idle
